# Quality Model

Quality is not one metric. It includes relevance, safety, freshness, diversity, exploration, fairness, latency, stability, and business fit.

A senior design optimises trustworthy improvement, not just score movement.

## Practical takeaway

This part of the design exists because recommendation systems must be explainable, safe, measurable, and recoverable.
